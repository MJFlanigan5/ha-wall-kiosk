Foyer interiors and Kith display: concepts

2026-11-10. Built from screenshots of live Foyer (192.168.2.25:8090) and Kith (192.168.2.45:7400), and the HaCasa / Fluvy motion values captured 11-07 (motion-reference-hacasa-fluvy.md). All animations on this page are real CSS, so what moves here is what would move on the wall.

1. Foyer: why the interior pages feel off

The ambient screen works because it is one system: a big monospace clock, a calm date line, and one grid of same-size tiles where every tile reads icon, state word, label. The room pages drop all of that. They switch to a left-aligned title, a stat strip, wide slider cards and a separate music card, each with its own size and spacing, on flat black. Nothing on them moves.

2. Foyer: proposed room page

Keep the ambient screen's grid and tile anatomy, and borrow three things from HaCasa: the big editorial room title, glass tiles over a slow warm gradient, and tiles that rise in. Every tile is the same unit as on the ambient screen. Lights get HaCasa's thin edge dimmer instead of a full-width slider, so they fit one tile.

4. Kith flashing: likely cause

In ~/hearth/src/main.jsx line 1153 the display picks the center panel by position: centerPanels[centerIdx % centerPanels.length]. That list is rebuilt from live data on every render. Music starts or stops (polled every 8 s), a widget's data drops out for a moment, or a message expires, and the list changes length. The same index then points at a different panel. That fires the fade-out/fade-in at lines 1154 to 1163 (opacity 0 for 380 ms) at random times, which reads as flashing.

Fix (not applied): track the panel by its id instead of its position. On each tick, find the current id in the new list and move to the next one. If it is gone, take whatever sits at that position now. Pair it with the slide transition above so a swap never goes to black. I have not seen the wall tablet itself, so confirm the flash happens around music starting or stopping before I change anything.
