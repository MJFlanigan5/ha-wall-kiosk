Kith display: options

Three layouts for the Kith wall display, drawn with Kith's real data as of Wednesday night, Nov 11. Today is Veterans Day, with AT&T and Sungage due. Clean House and Feed Johnny are due Friday, the lawn and dryer lint Saturday, trash on Sunday. A USPS package is due Friday. No dinners are planned and the grocery list is empty. Clarkston weather from Homey: 55° tonight, light rain tomorrow.

Two problems every option fixes

  • The flash. The middle panel is picked by its position in a list that changes size whenever music starts or stops, or a widget drops out for a moment. The same position then points at a different panel, which fades to black and back. The fix is to track the panel by name instead of position, and slide between panels instead of fading to black.
  • 75 packages stuck "in transit". Kith logs Amazon's "Delivered" emails as new in-transit packages and never marks them delivered, so the list goes back to October. Anything showing a package count is wrong until that's fixed. Really, only a few are in transit right now.

Today's layout, kept: upcoming on the left, one rotating panel in the middle, weather on the right.
