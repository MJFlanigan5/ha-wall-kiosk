Things to decide

  • Layout: A, B or C, or a mix (for example B's status line with A's tiles).
  • Light colour: the light's own colour, green or amber. If you choose the light's own colour, the inner room pages switch from amber to it too, so it's the same everywhere.
  • What B and C drop: the water tiles and the Hot water button. Those stay on the Energy page, one tap away. The timer and theme buttons in the corners stay in all three.
